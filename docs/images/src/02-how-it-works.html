How it works

From a sip on stream to a paid creator, in seconds.

01

◉Watch

The stream is cut into 10 second clips. Each clip carries its video, its audio and the live chat posted during it.

Twitch · YouTube · webcam · ffmpeg
02

✦Detect

Gemini returns structured moments: product on camera, drinking, spoken mentions, brand screen time and safety flags.

Gemini Flash · JSON schema
03

⊘Decide

The tip policy blocks sarcasm, competitors, replays, staged or unsafe moments and repeats. Every skip keeps its reason.

Tip policy · cooldowns · caps
04

✓Verify

A stronger model re-checks each candidate like a sceptical auditor, and scores how chat reacted. Hype raises the tip.

Gemini Pro · up to 1.5× for chat
05

$Pay

The tip is reserved against the campaign budget under a row lock, then sent as an idempotent Stripe transfer.

Supabase ledger · Stripe Connect
06

★Show

A "paid placement" thank-you alert lands on stream, so the brand gets a second moment in front of viewers.

Realtime · Gemini TTS + card
